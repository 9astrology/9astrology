2 About

A practice rooted in śāstra, offered with quiet care.

For over a decade, Purvi has practiced classical Vedic astrology from her home in Mumbai, sitting with clients across cities and continents. Her readings draw on the Parāśara tradition — patient, exacting, and always anchored in what's actually written in the chart.

She came to jyotiṣa not as a believer but as a sceptic with too many coincidences to ignore. Today she works equally with skeptics and seekers, and her sessions tend to feel less like prophecy and more like a long, clarifying conversation about a life you already know.

Beyond the chart, she reads palms, shuffles tarot when the moment asks for it, and finds quiet music in the numbers behind a name.

7 Questions

A few things often asked.

How long is a typical consultation?

Most readings run between forty-five minutes and an hour. Compatibility and life-overview sessions can run longer; brief follow-ups are shorter. We'll agree on the format when you book.

Are sessions online or in person?

Almost all consultations are online — over Google Meet or WhatsApp video — which keeps things easy across timezones. Mumbai-based clients who prefer to meet in person can request that when booking.

What languages do you offer?

English, Hindi, and Gujarati. Mention your preference at the time of booking and the session will be conducted accordingly.

What information should I prepare?

Your date of birth, exact time of birth (as accurate as possible — even a few minutes can shift a chart), and place of birth. If you are coming with a specific question, jot it down. That's all.

How is payment handled?

Payment is taken at the time of booking, via UPI, bank transfer, or card. International clients can pay via Wise or PayPal. Receipts are emailed after the session.
